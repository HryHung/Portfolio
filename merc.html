MERC 2025.

Mobile Lifter and Gripper Robot

OVERVIEW

An operator-controlled competition robot combining a mobile chassis, vertical lift, and dual grippers for object handling. Mechanical actuation and embedded electronics bring movement, lifting, and gripping into one physical platform.

01

A mobile base with guided vertical movement

Four GA25 motors form the drivetrain. Above the base, a slider rail guides the lifting assembly while a belt-and-pulley mechanism supplies vertical actuation. The CAD assembly and side-view photograph show how the lifting column is packaged with the mobile chassis.

The distinction between guidance and actuation is visible in this architecture: the rail defines the travel path, while the belt transmits motion. The supplied note identifies the components but does not provide a measured lift height or cycle time.

02

Dual gripping & operator-controlled electronics

A dual-gripper arrangement uses GJA worm-geared motors. Together with the mobile base and lift, the grippers provide the mechanisms needed for operator-controlled object handling.

Electrical integration combines two Arduino Mini controllers, two drivers, and two relays. A Mini-C6 six-channel controller provides operator input. The project note also lists UART communication, but does not document the detailed signal architecture or allocation of tasks between the two controllers. The portfolio therefore avoids assuming an undocumented control topology.
