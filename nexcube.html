DESIGN / BUILD / EVALUATE

Engineering Approach

The concept addresses the wiring and assembly overhead of separate tools in a small electronics workspace. The technical disclosure concentrates on the reusable connection system; the product photographs and CV document four functional prototypes.

01

Four tools, one modular workstation

The Battery, Tester, Power, and Soldering modules each package a practical workstation function. The portfolio describes an 18650 battery assembly with charging and battery management, an LCR component tester, an adjustable power module, and a soldering module using a custom PCBA.

Rather than requiring a separate enclosure and connection scheme for every tool, the project develops a common physical format. The CV records housing, wiring-bus, and PCBA development for all four prototypes. The assembled product shows the architecture brought into physical form, while the team presentation places that work in its development context.

02

Separating alignment from electrical contact

The adapter sits between a power module and a functional module, or between successive modules. Magnets assist positioning; guide surfaces and keyed or asymmetric geometry support the intended orientation. The contact layout provides a further mechanical–electrical constraint against incorrect mating.

Spring contacts, such as pogo pins, compress against mating pads when the adapter reaches its working position. That contact pressure is the electrical connection mechanism; magnetic positioning is a separate function. A positive-supply and ground bus can pass power onward through successive modules without a separate supply adapter for each unit.

03

Enclosure development & evidence boundaries

Printed enclosure development integrates the external connection features with the internal electronics. SolidWorks design and Bambu Lab print preparation support iteration on the module housings and adapter fit. The physical modules establish prototype assembly, not a quantified reduction in setup time or a durability rating.

The disclosure documents drawings, component descriptions, operating sequences, and draft claims for a potential patent or utility-solution application. Electrical protection, electronic identification, and controlled power switching are optional configurations in that document, not confirmed prototype features. No connection-cycle, contact-resistance, current-capacity, or thermal test is supplied.
